About Me

Mathematics graduate passionate about data analytics,
business intelligence, and turning numbers into decisions.

I am Mehedi Hasan, a Mathematics graduate from Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University (MBSTU), Bangladesh. My academic background in mathematics gave me a strong foundation in logical thinking and problem-solving — skills I now apply to real-world data challenges.

I specialize in end-to-end data analytics: from raw data cleaning and exploratory analysis in Python to interactive dashboards in Tableau and structured querying in SQL. I have built projects spanning retail sales, HR analytics, customer churn, credit risk, and global layoff trends — each following a complete workflow from data to insight.

I am open to full-time roles in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, and MIS in Dhaka — as well as freelance projects where I can deliver data-driven insights and interactive dashboards.
